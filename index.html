The Wheelhouse Workshop is one shop where every project is handpicked, built from inside the communities it belongs to, and handed back to them.

AWhy it matters·NTS

The marriage of digital and human.

Screens hold our records and our time, and too little of it comes back into real life. The Workshop marries digital and human, positively and meaningfully — so what lives on a screen comes back as something you keep, a room worth being in, a friendship made at a show.

BWhat it is

One shop. Every project handpicked.

A creative and technology studio in Oakland: one shop, every project handpicked — games, software, digital media. The high concept is the marriage of digital and human, enriching personal and professional lives. Each spoke serves a community the Workshop already belongs to, across generations.

DETAIL B1 · MUSIC LIVE

Dead Set

A guide that knows every show the Grateful Dead ever played. Tell it your mood and it builds you tonight’s concert — built by and for the community, and strictly non-commercial. It is also the Workshop’s living workshop: what it teaches about building community becomes practice everywhere else.

Built on the shoulders of the tapers, the traders, and the Internet Archive.

DHow·Operating model·NTS

Pre-tensioned: light, fast, built to carry weight.

  1. 1

    HUB · THE FOUNDER

    Torque, not micromanagement. Vision, funding and priorities travel out through the spokes; the hub never pushes the rim.

  2. 2

    SPOKES · THE PROJECTS

    Held in tension by deadlines, KPIs and scope. Cross-laced, so ventures share the load; laced in silos, they twist and snap.

  3. 3

    RIM · THE CRAFT

    A rotating ring of expert craftspeople, held true by the projects’ tension. They take the compression of delivery.

  4. 4

    TIRE · THE MARKET

    Where the Workshop meets people. It flexes with feedback, and shows any misalignment upstream as uneven wear.

EWhat the shop stands for

A conscience, and a cycle. Impact counted in lives, not quarters.

  1. A SEAT AT THE BENCH

    The best rooms hold more than one generation. The young see what the rest of us have stopped noticing; experience knows where the road bends. There is always a seat for someone younger and hungrier.

  2. A SHARED PASSION IS A PASSPORT

    Loving the same thing dissolves the lines between people faster than anything else. Bikes, games, music: none of them ask where you’re from.

  3. ONE OF ONE

    Unique, artisanal, precious. Made to be kept and handed down.

  4. PUT BACK TO WORK

    The Workshop measures itself by what it leaves in the communities it belongs to. Whatever it earns is put back to work, funding the next contribution and the one after it.
